PILOT BRIEFING

Know your controls.

Helicopters: Raise collective above 50% to lift off. Gently press W to accelerate; use Q/E to turn in a hover. Reduce collective to descend.

Airplanes: Raise throttle, release brake, gain runway speed, then hold S gently to rotate. Use A/D to bank and turn. Slow down with flaps for landing; excessive pitch at low speed causes a stall.

A simplified simulator for exploration, not a training device. Cockpit camera includes a procedural panel; aircraft systems and aerodynamic models are approximations.
