# Original concept cars

Fifteen original fictional concept models authored in Blender for SpeakKai in October 2026. These are not replicas or official manufacturer products. All performance values are authored game inputs with separate competition tuning; their measured model geometry supplies wheelbase and tyre placement.

- Pack A: Asterion Blade, Helix Sprint, Ion Arrow.
- Pack B: Vesper GT, Tempest R, Atlas Endurance.
- Pack C: Kestrel Formula, Apex Canopy, Vector Groundforce.
- Pack D: Aurora E, Flux R, Solstice Streamliner.
- Pack E: Obsidian V12, Cinder Rally GT, Meridian Hybrid.

Each model preserves independent named major body panels, hub-centred wheel assemblies, rim/tire components, brakes, suspension details, lights and aerodynamic parts. Editable Blender sources, authoring scripts and design references are retained in the project. Legacy third-party car model credits remain in the linked car attribution files.
