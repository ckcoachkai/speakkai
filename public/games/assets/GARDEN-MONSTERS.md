# Sourced garden creature animation

Downloaded character pixels are rendered by games/garden-monsters.js. Source URLs, original timelines, interpolation, rectangles, hashes and rights are in garden-sourced/manifest.json. No replacement character is hand-drawn or AI-generated.

Every active picker lane independently permits giant spawns after the first wave, with one living giant per lane. Regular zombie stats never scale with time; queued zombies create difficulty. Giant height is224logical pixels versus112for normal bodies (2times), with ordinary zombie size unchanged. Giant clips span the shared lawn in front of lawn actors, with student tags painted last; a ground marker identifies their target lane.

Dog Man, Hello Kitty, Pikachu and Niu Lai face the player column on the left. Dog Man is no longer mirrored away. Kitty uses a genuine profile/three-quarter pose, retaining its original magnifying glass; detached question marks were removed. Pikachu's detailed native407×339 movie artwork replaces the old60pxsprite, with smooth image scaling. Niu Lai uses actual opening-movie up-left profile frames.

Combat stays at fixed40ms steps while painted positions and clocks interpolate at display refresh. Eight source cycles have baked optical-flow frames targeting60fps; the duck retains150authored frames at30fps. Separate instance playheads and100ms transitions preserve feet and timing without consuming combat RNG.

## October17 foreground and Minecraft update

Names and health bars are beside student avatars, within the student column. Large creatures paint in front of the lawn actors, while player labels remain legible. Regular zombies retain112logical-pixelreferenceheight; large monsters224; the miniature Minecraft Zombie72. All six players remain visible.

Minecraft textures, cuboid models, UVs and animation formulas come from Mojang. Locally baked32frame walking/attack poses use nearest-neighbor sampling, not replacement drawings. A friendly villager watches near the house and uses three original ambient OGG samples from Mojang’s verified resource CDN; sound follows the existing mute/lifecycle controls. The villager does not attack students. Exact source URLs and hashes are in garden-sourced/minecraft-sources.json.

Demolisher and Mutated Zombie use original7DaysToDie official reveal/screenshot pixels. Their small head/arm/leg movements are locally articulated stills, not native game animation. Full silhouettes overlap the joints to prevent cut-off gaps. Demolisher has a3simulation-second contact fuse,260localplantdamage and no direct student blast damage. The irradiated mutant is explicitly a custom radiation effect on the complete Mutated Zombie body, not an official irradiated Screamer model. Combat values are fixed classroom adaptations; ordinary PvZ family statistics never scale with time.
