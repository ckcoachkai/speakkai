# Sourced garden creature animation

The game draws downloaded character pixels through `games/garden-monsters.js`. No AI-generated or procedurally redrawn character replaces the source artwork. The manifest records source URLs, native frame counts, processing, hashes and source-specific rights.

Combat stays at deterministic40ms steps. Zombies, projectiles and mowers interpolate only their painted positions at display refresh; plants and characters use smooth presentation clocks. Baked bidirectional optical-flow frames bring eight sourced cycles to approximately60fps without browser-time image processing. The duck retains150authored frames at30fps. Each live creature has its own playhead and100ms transitions; timing never consumes combat RNG. Native pixel-art scaling remains explicit.

Ordinary zombie size stays `clamp((laneHeight-4)/113,0.20,1)`. Giants use370logical pixels versus112for ordinary bodies and occupy the shared lawn behind foreground actors and names. Arena bounds keep them onscreen; ground markers identify the target lane. Only loaded source assets spawn. Regular zombie HP/armor/speed/damage never grow with time or wave: difficulty comes from additional queued zombies. Giants have separate fixed higher HP, with no scripted wipe.

The original source pixels are decoded, uniformly cropped or masked, aligned to consistent cells, and compressed into transparent WebP atlases. Interpolated frames are labeled separately from native poses. Niu Lai has a movie talking/head-lift loop rather than walking, with documented crown/horn/hoof source limits. Dog Man uses a short three-pose movie run, not a complete action set. Team Rocket trainers remain posed beside animated Meowth. Ultraman has dedicated walking, idle, attack, hit and collapse clips. Other characters retain their available source cycle with hit feedback and a defeat transform.
