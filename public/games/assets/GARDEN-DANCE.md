# Garden dance update — 2026-10-18

Animal players have six original Canvas damage appearances (healthy plus five damage stages), with clipped cartoon bite notches and increasing fear. Main canvas is never erased. All ten species have long-limbed adult-proportioned celebration rigs with connected elbows and knees. At 120 BPM the 16-count square sequence uses right/left step touches, heel digs and arm sweeps; the alternate shuffle uses heel/toe pivots and knee lifts. The speaker panel lasts 16 seconds and has Continue; reduced-motion preference gives a still pose. Game speed does not accelerate these dances.
