Runtime hovering, projectiles and explosions are separately animated; the illustrations are single poses. Clear-color variants are rendered opaque for game readability. ## Zombie rubber duck

`garden-sourced/zombie-duck.webp`: four OpenAI image-generated left-facing waddle poses, from exec-4259010e-9238-4f52-8163-b822ee2e2a4b.png. Prompt direction: vibrant yellow rubber duck with cloudy eyes, stitches, cracked rubber and green seams; transparent background, non-graphic zombie variant. This replaces the former CC0 illustration; the former source attribution and hashes do not describe this replacement.

## Clone overhead slam

`garden-allies/clone-slam.webp`: 120 articulated poses, 300 by 300 each; first 60 dragging walk, next 60 overhead raise, downward slam, recovery. Produced by `work/air-support/build-slam.mjs` from existing sourced armor parts and a custom sword, with 20 ms frame durations. Existing armor reference: https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phase_II_clone_trooper_armor . Custom joint motion is not official Star Wars animation. Blade raise adjusted for transparent padding above the sword tip.

## Sky visitors
Haku dragon and No-Face spirit are generated interpretations with true alpha transparency, not official film frames. Three varied-scale copies of each float through the sky strip with independent travel speeds and bobbing. Generated sources: exec-1165f3ae-aa0d-4439-91d9-a49d0b9afa24.png, exec-eeb65966-8789-4c13-8b7c-65a4e973c467.png.

Combat sounds: short original synthesized rifle cracks, laser zaps and low-pass explosion noise; winner dance loop is original 120 BPM synthesized music. Existing game samples retain their prior provenance.
