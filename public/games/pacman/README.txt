KAI-MAN & PAC-MAN — SPEAKKAI GAMES

Open index.html in a browser. No installation or network is needed.

Move the pointer to the top, left or right edge to reveal a menu. Click an edge tab to keep a panel open; close it with its X or Escape. Opening panels pauses the chase. Arrow keys control the character, never the menus.

Games & mode lets you choose Kai-Man (Kai's supplied photo turned into a two-frame transparent cartoon sprite) or original yellow Pac-Man. Choose Normal game or Name chooser.

Normal: 3 lives, 4 ghosts, 244 pellets, 10 points per dot, 50 per power pellet. Blue ghosts give 200/400/800/1600 points; their eyes return home and they rejoin. Clear the maze to advance levels. Lost lives keep cleared dots. Extra life at 10,000 points, fruit bonuses and browser-local high score. Fan-made recreation rather than original ROM emulation.

Name chooser: enter 1–60 names, one per line. Big pellets turn students blue for six seconds. Only blue students can be selected. Colored collision shows Kai died and picks nobody. A catch pauses on the student's name until Next chase. The next chase has a fresh maze and one fewer ghost. Duplicate names remain distinct students.

Arrow keys / WASD / direction pad / swipes. Space pauses. Full screen includes the menus and controls. Neon and classic maze styles. Names, turns and high score are saved only in this browser.

Kai sprite: OpenAI image generation using Kai's supplied portrait as identity reference. Prompt: two aligned transparent cartoon head frames, smiling and eating, preserving curly quiff and facial features, crisp arcade sprite edges.

Pac-Man original maze/characters/audio: Namco / Bandai Namco. Sounds: https://www.classicgaming.cc/classics/pac-man/sounds
History: https://www.pacman.com/en/history/

Side-profile Kai and Trump-Man use aligned closed/open mouth sprite frames. Characters face the travel direction and chomp while moving. Choose either character or original Pac-Man from Games & mode. The enlarged Down button supports both pointer input and click, highlights the queued direction and turns at the next open passage.
