# SpeakKai Speaker Arcade

Published at https://speakkai.com/games/ with five full-screen name pickers and a playable daytime lawn-defense mode. Kai-Man and Pac-Man run natively inside the same arcade, sharing its class roster, speaking order and edge controls.

- Name Wheel: blue, red, yellow and green, large centered wheel, equal slices, and a selected-slice explosion. Click the hub or Play.
- Mouse Maze:43×29 maze; every mouse starts at the same top-left cell0,0 and the exit is bottom-right42,28. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, pinch on touch. Cheese increases size, speed and aura, insects add20 damage stages, bombs are local, and a cat arrives at20 seconds. Animal horror effects remain. Exit opens at27 seconds.
- Plants vs. Zombies: classic pea-shooter plant faces protect students at the far left. Each student starts with150HP behind a300HP pea shooter. Suns recruit or evolve distinct shooters, up to5 living defenders per lane (4shooters plus a real blocker); zombie last-hit loot recruits or upgrades them, and stars enhance the team. Every defender has its own health and fires independently. Zombies must defeat all living defenders before passing the single-use lawnmower between the student and plants. On the first breach, it automatically sweeps the lane and clears every zombie it intersects, including armored variants. It stays spent until the next round, when each student receives a fresh mower. The first breach cannot damage the student; later zombies can after the mower passes. The first student's HP to reach0 selects that name, followed by a non-graphic stun animation and winner popup. There are100 custom zombie builds and100 loot items, with12 shooter styles plus Wall-nut, Tall-nut and a custom thorny chestnut blocker. Standard pea-family volleys now take about1.43seconds; Melon-pult and Kernel-pult fire about every2.93seconds. Repeater fires a spaced pair, Threepeater attacks neighboring rows, Gatling fires four shots, Snow Pea slows walking and biting for10seconds, Melon-pult deals80direct damage plus nearby splash, and Kernel-pult sometimes stuns with40-damage butter. Winter Melon combines splash with chilling. Upgrades preserve the calmer firing pace. Legacy fire, laser and thorn loot IDs now use Repeater, Gatling and Cactus attacks; the thorny chestnut remains a picker extension.
- Classic garden: choose Classic game under Games, or open https://speakkai.com/games/?game=garden&mode=classic. Defend a5×9 lawn through three finite waves using15 seed packets, manual25-sun collection, Sunflowers, actual seed costs/recharges, shovel, keyboard/touch planting, pause/resume and one mower per row. Gatling upgrades Repeater; Winter Melon upgrades Melon-pult. Classic uses source-backed plant HP, projectile behavior, armor, shield and zombie data. Winning or losing never picks a student or changes speaking order. This is a browser adaptation with an authored three-wave level, not the original fifty-level campaign.
- Grand Prix: circuit fills96.5%width and90%height. Identical cars have shuffled grid positions, spread across3 broad lanes; crashes, spins, oil, cones, fuel boosts and recovery affect the actual first finisher. Realistic top-down sprites; lower hazard density preserves open space.
- Bear's Teeth: PG candy-bear and R horror-bear. Students grip actual teeth/candies in the artwork. A polygon-clipped fragment of the same tooth pulls out of its shaded socket.14candy anchors/12horror anchors; larger classes share physical anchors. Students fall onto a cushion; horror blood belongs to the bear and teeth.

The entire viewport is the arena. Reveal edge drawers: games above, names right, speaking order left, controls below. Click a tab to pin it; close with × or Escape. Space starts a round; F toggles browser fullscreen. Sound is optional and initially muted.

Edit up to60 names. Give everyone a turn removes picked names; Undo restores the last speaker. The popup dismisses and resets the game after3.2seconds; Garden uses5.6seconds to let its outro cue finish. Names and order are saved only in this browser.

Wheel, maze and bear reveal a uniform crypto-random speaker. Garden and race use symmetric starting specifications, shuffled assignments and actual simulation results. Garden rounds progress through ordinary zombie waves, with8%stronger bites and gentle increases in wave pressure; there is no timed giant-zombie wipe or selection deadline. Race rounds finish around10–12seconds. Greater than10 garden players scroll vertically; narrow screens scroll the620px lawn horizontally.

Garden now renders original jointed parts and animation tracks instead of the generated full-body plant/zombie atlases. The older atlases remain fallbacks. The Names drawer imports16SpeakKai class rosters live from /data/feedback.json. Clear, consistent he/she subject references in each student’s written feedback suggest a fictional portrait; missing or mixed references remain unspecified. The game does not infer gender from a name or claim a student’s gender identity. Every portrait is editable, saved by class-scoped student ID, and preserved when switching classes. Only names, portrait choices and speaking order are saved in the browser. Feedback prose is not copied into this project.
