    A LITTLE CLASSROOM MAGIC

    How to play

    Top: choose a game. Right: edit your class. Left: see the speaking order. Bottom: game controls.

    Move to an edge to reveal its panel. Tap or click an edge tab to pin the panel. Close it with ×, tap the tab again, or press Escape.

    Press play or Space to choose the next speaker. Press F for browser fullscreen.

    After each result, the chosen player is removed from all name pickers. The Names drawer and its counter show only players still in the game. Use Undo to restore the last player or Reset Order to bring everyone back. Every eligible player has an equal chance. Starting cars and garden teams are assigned fairly; collisions, loot, and combat determine their results. In Name picker mode, suns recruit or improve shooters, zombie loot rewards the last hit, and stars power up the team. Wall-nuts, Tall-nuts and thorny chestnuts block bites while the shooters fight. Plants have five visible health stages; zombies keep their original body type as armor is damaged. In Names, import your class from SpeakKai feedback and choose each student’s portrait. Enable ♪ in Controls for the opening cue, battle sounds and selection music. In Plants vs. Zombies, each student has one lawnmower between them and their plants. If zombies breach the defenders, it automatically sweeps through that lane once. Later zombies can reach the student after the mower is spent. A fresh mower returns next round. The first student to lose all HP speaks. The wheel, maze, and bear reveal random picks.

    Choose Classic game under Games to defend a five-row lawn yourself. Start the battle, collect sun, choose a seed packet, then click or tap an empty lawn square. Arrow keys move between squares; Enter or Space plants the selected seed. Sunflowers produce sun. Gatling Pea upgrades a Repeater; Winter Melon upgrades a Melon-pult. Use the shovel to remove a plant, and Pause to stop the battle. Snow Pea slows walking and biting; Melon-pult splashes nearby zombies; Kernel-pult sometimes stuns with butter. Use Game pace in Controls to slow or speed up either garden mode, including during a battle. Lawnmowers sweep silently; suns play a gentle rising chime when sound is enabled. In the name picker, suns also recruit Potato Mines, Torchwood and Cherry Bombs. Mines need time to arm, bombs clear nearby lanes, and Torchwood ignites passing peas. Every lane faces growing waves of 5, 50, 100, 200 and beyond. Classic offers the same plants and an Endless survival option. Protect the house through three waves in the standard game. Each row has one mower rescue. This mode keeps your speaking order intact.

    Kai-Man: move with arrows, WASD or the direction buttons. Collect a power pellet and catch a blue student ghost to choose the next speaker. Pac-Man: clear the maze and keep your speaking order intact. Both games use this arcade’s Names, Controls and Speaking order panels.

    Your names and speaking order are saved in this browser. The maze includes cartoon animal blood and defeat effects. Bear defaults to cuddly candy mode; choose R · Horror in Controls for the frightening bear artwork.
