# Human reaction recordings

All six included recordings are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Author credit is retained here even where not required. These sources do not imply endorsement.

- **male-scream.wav** — JohnsonBrandEditing; excerpt prepared by congusbongus. [Source](https://freesound.org/people/JohnsonBrandEditing/sounds/173944/). Human male scream; selected CC0 portion 3 of the mixed-license collection. Excerpt 0–0.840s; mono 24kHz PCM16; high/low-pass filtering; loudness normalization; 12ms attack and 75ms tail fades. In-game pitch/volume/panning vary per actor.
- **female-scream.wav** — AuraVoice / Nocturnal_Vanguard. [Source](https://opengameart.org/content/female-scream-1). Recorded scream supplied by a professional voice-over artist. Excerpt 0–1.700s; mono 24kHz PCM16; high/low-pass filtering; loudness normalization; 12ms attack and 75ms tail fades. In-game pitch/volume/panning vary per actor.
- **male-gasp.wav** — jawbutch. [Source](https://freesound.org/people/jawbutch/sounds/344407/). Male Gasp 1, source tagged man/male; official public high-quality preview. Excerpt 0–0.666s; mono 24kHz PCM16; high/low-pass filtering; loudness normalization; 12ms attack and 75ms tail fades. In-game pitch/volume/panning vary per actor.
- **female-gasp.wav** — cicifyre. [Source](https://opengameart.org/content/voice-clip-packs-for-visual-novels-and-rpgs). Gasp from the explicitly Mature Female voice pack. Excerpt 0–0.846s; mono 24kHz PCM16; high/low-pass filtering; loudness normalization; 12ms attack and 75ms tail fades. In-game pitch/volume/panning vary per actor.
- **female-effort.wav** — cicifyre. [Source](https://opengameart.org/content/voice-clip-packs-for-visual-novels-and-rpgs). Short acted effort/exclamation from the Mature Female pack, used for mild hurt reactions. Excerpt 0–0.846s; mono 24kHz PCM16; high/low-pass filtering; loudness normalization; 12ms attack and 75ms tail fades. In-game pitch/volume/panning vary per actor.
- **soft-cry.wav** — AlucardsBride. [Source](https://freesound.org/people/AlucardsBride/sounds/172738/). Short excerpt of a woman crying softly with trembling breaths, from the official public high-quality preview. Excerpt 4.28–6.080s; mono 24kHz PCM16; high/low-pass filtering; loudness normalization; 12ms attack and 75ms tail fades. In-game pitch/volume/panning vary per actor.

The male scream uses only aargh3.ogg, identified as CC0 in the collection's aargh.txt. None of that collection's CC-BY files are included. Original source pages, archive notices, and download receipts are preserved in the development evidence.
