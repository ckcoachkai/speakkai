PAC-MAN CLASSROOM — NAME PICKER

Open index.html in Edge or Chrome. No installation is needed. Keep the assets,
engine.js, game.js, and style.css alongside index.html.

1. Enter one student name per line (1–60 students), then press LET'S PLAY.
2. Move Pac-Man with the arrow keys, W A S D, the direction buttons, or swipes.
3. Eat a large power pellet. The first BLUE student ghost you touch is selected. The chase pauses and their
   name appears in the turn list.
4. Press NEXT CHASE to play with the remaining students. Names do not repeat.
5. Reset turns begins a fresh selection order. Edit lets you change the roster.
   Changing the roster resets the selection order; an unchanged roster keeps it.

Power pellets grant six seconds of faster movement and turn all ghosts blue.
Blue ghosts flee at varied speeds. Colored ghosts chase you; touching one shows
"Kai died" and lets you retry with no student picked. Only blue ghosts are eligible.
The original arcade rules are adapted for name picking.
Spawn locations are shuffled on each chase. This is a game-controlled picker,
so your steering can affect who gets selected.

Space pauses or resumes. Sound can be muted at the top. Full screen keeps the
maze and direction buttons together; press Escape to leave full screen.

The roster and turn list are stored in this browser when browser storage is
available. Student data is not uploaded by this game. Clearing browser storage
or switching browsers removes or separates the saved class.
