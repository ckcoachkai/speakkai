    How it works
    Eat a large power pellet to turn the ghosts blue. Catch a blue ghost to pick a student. Touch a colored ghost and Kai dies — no student is picked.

    Arcade credits

    A classroom adaptation of Pac-Man’s maze and characters. Colored ghosts chase Kai. Power pellets turn them blue, and catching a blue ghost adds a student to the turn list. This is a fan-made name picker.
