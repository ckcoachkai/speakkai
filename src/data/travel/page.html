The full introduction

AI narration · ElevenLabs

One night. Two people. Fifteen hundred yuan. Can we escape Shanghai, spend an afternoon swimming, and still be home before noon tomorrow? Here's the plan!

Wednesday, September sixteenth. Ten a.m.: leave Qibao. Check traffic and allow roughly ninety minutes to two hours. Around noon, arrive at Hualuxe Suzhou Bay, leave the bags, and get lunch. At one, the hardest decision is tea, a walk, or absolutely nothing.

Three p.m.: check in. Three thirty: swimming and poolside rest. No racing between attractions. Just one very important mission: relax.

But check before booking! Pool hours, swim caps, access for both guests, and breakfast need confirming. Early access isn't guaranteed. Spa treatments and hot spring tickets are separate.

Five p.m.: your choice. Quiet time for free, or an optional spa treatment after confirming the price and availability. Six thirty: dinner. Eight: a gentle stroll if conditions suit, a cup of tea, and an early night.

Now, can we hit the budget? Our starting estimate is eight hundred fifty yuan for the room, three hundred fifty for food, and two hundred for return driving. That's fourteen hundred total. One hundred under target! Add the provisional three hundred yuan spa allowance and it becomes seventeen hundred. These are estimates, not live quotes. Breakfast is assumed included; budget extra if it isn't.

Thursday, September seventeenth. Seven fifteen: breakfast. Eight: one last coffee or short walk. Eight forty-five: check out. Nine: leave. Aim to reach Qibao around ten thirty to eleven, with extra time before the noon deadline. Traffic gets the final say, so leave earlier if needed.

Your turn! Open the itinerary, adjust the budget, explore the three-D journey, and save your plan. Pack your ID, charger, swimwear, swim cap, and booking confirmation. Nothing is booked yet. The hotel photos are real; the dreamy lake art and three-D route are illustrations. Now let's trade one busy day for a little breathing room!

一晚，两个人，预算一千五！能不能离开上海，痛快游个泳，第二天中午前还赶回家？这趟苏州湾小度假，现在出发！

九月十六日，周三上午十点，从七宝出发。先看实时路况，单程预留一个半到两个小时。中午十二点左右到苏州湾华邑温泉度假酒店，寄存行李，吃个午饭。下午一点，喝茶、散步，或者什么都不干。

三点入住，三点半游泳、池畔休息。今天的任务只有一个：把自己放松下来！预订前记得确认泳池时间、泳帽要求、双人使用权限和早餐。提前使用设施不保证，温泉门票和护理也不是一回事。

五点，你来选！安静休息，或者确认价格和预约后，加一场护理。六点半晚餐，八点看情况散个步，喝杯茶，早点睡。

重点来了，一千五够不够？暂按房费八百五、双人餐饮三百五、往返自驾两百算，总共一千四，还剩一百！如果加上暂定三百元的护理，就变成一千七。这些是规划估算，不是实时价格。暂按含早餐计算，不含就要另加预算。

周四，九月十七日，七点十五吃早餐。八点再喝杯咖啡，或者走一小圈。八点四十五退房，九点出发。争取十点半到十一点回到七宝，为中午前到家留足时间。遇到拥堵，就提前走！

现在轮到你了！展开行程，调整预算，转动三维路线，再收藏你的计划。带上证件、充电器、泳衣泳帽，确认好预订。提醒一下，目前还没订房。酒店照片是实景，梦幻湖景和三维路线是艺术示意。准备好了吗？把忙碌留在身后，去湖边，慢下来！
