# SpeakKai brand guidelines — V30

Current identity and media rules. See HANDOVER.md and the source manifests for the release inventory.

## Identity

SpeakKai / 说开. Kai Liu · Communication coach & speaker. Primary invitation: **Make yourself understood.** Method: **Clear thinking. Purposeful practice. Human connection.** Current scope: public speaking education, keynotes and team training; Shanghai, online and by arrangement.

Write as a practical coach speaking to a person with a real communication task. Name what the speaker can try and what the listener can notice. Use an encouraging, direct voice without guaranteed transformation. Parents need age/scope and a concrete practice example; schools need fit and responsibilities; companies need the communication challenge and a clear discussion route.

The published career figures—15+ years, 1,000+ presentations across 7 countries, 2,000+ students trained—are first-party claims. They are not independent evidence of learning or client outcomes.

## Visual identity

Use the tokens and component rules in DESIGN-SYSTEM.md. Ink #0b1020, blue #2454d8, yellow #ffcc00, white #ffffff, wash #f0f3f8, muted #535e72, line #dbe1ec. Blue carries actions and emphasis; yellow is a restrained accent. Local Manrope weights 400/600/700 serve English text; preserve system fallbacks for other scripts. Shared width is 1220px and spacing uses clamp(24px, 5vw, 72px).

Keep the logo proportional and legible. The header source of truth is src/assets/brand/speakkai-logo.png, transformed by Astro Image into responsive WebP. Do not redraw or stretch it. Keep clear space from surrounding text and controls; do not turn the mark into an endorsement or certification seal.

## Factual and editorial boundaries

Keep course facts synchronized through src/data/currentProgram.ts: Young Competition Speakers, Fall 2026, Grades 1–2, 18 classes, six three-class units, 60–90-second practice speeches and a supported three-minute final showcase. Do not transfer this scope to other programs by implication.

Fictional teaching examples stay explicitly fictional and adjacent to that label. Do not manufacture testimonials, student/client results, partnerships, credentials, awards, rankings, certificates, fixed fees or availability. A draft, schedule view or inquiry is not a booking. A photo or social handle does not imply endorsement. English/Chinese content must preserve meaning and scope; editor notes remain private. Identifiable student material requires appropriate permission.

## Release review

Check actual mobile/desktop reading, contrast combinations, keyboard focus, motion preferences and asset size. Review text in context. Keep objective test results separate from heuristic design scores and unmeasured trust, conversion and learning effects.
