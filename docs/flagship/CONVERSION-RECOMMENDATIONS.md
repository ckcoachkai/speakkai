# Conversion recommendations — working reference through V20

Reconcile after V30. No visitor analytics, controlled experiment, conversion uplift or booking result is established. A heuristic cycle score is not a conversion metric. Local copy success does not mean an inquiry was sent.

## Begin with consented task observation

Ask participants from each audience to choose an appropriate offer, inspect an example and explain the next step. Record anonymous task success, wrong turns, time to find information and the details they still need. Use general scenarios, not actual student records or confidential company briefs. A small formative study can reveal issues; it does not establish population conversion rates.

## Three candidate comparisons

| Question | Compare | Observe | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Can visitors find their starting point? | Current audience shortcuts/cards against a clearly defined alternative placement or label | Correct offer choice, wrong routes, time to choice | Do not infer trust or enrollment from a click |
| Do examples make the method understandable? | V19 homepage against V20 example links | Successful destination arrival and a participant's explanation of the next practice step | Comprehension is not learning improvement |
| Does preselection help school inquiries? | Generic school contact start against direction-prefilled path | Correct program direction, independent delivery preference, draft review and copy/manual-selection completion | Do not collect text or infer a WeChat send/booking |

Test one primary hypothesis at a time. Define the audience, primary outcome, observation window and guardrails before comparing versions. Keep the existing champion unless evidence supports the challenger and release gates pass. Low traffic may favor moderated observation over a numerical A/B test. If a statistical experiment becomes appropriate, choose sample size and stopping rules from baseline rates and the smallest meaningful effect; do not invent those inputs.

## Event boundaries if analytics is later authorized

Prefer anonymous aggregate route/action counts. Do not collect inquiry text, clipboard content, student names, personal records, full query strings, recordings or confidential company material. Do not install cross-site identifiers or advertising pixels merely to obtain a conversion number. Explain any collection and apply the appropriate consent and retention choices before deploying it.

Potential events are audience-route choice, example-link activation, practice start/completion and local draft/copy outcome. They measure website actions only. Link them to actual commercial outcomes only through a separately authorized, privacy-reviewed process with a clear definition of inquiry, qualified lead and booking.

## Practical priorities

Keep direct WeChat available, make fees/delivery/availability discussion explicit and preserve truthful clipboard failure recovery. Improve a confusing path before adding more calls to action. Obtain permission and exact wording for any future testimonial or case story. Stronger proof requires real evidence, not visual polish or fictional examples.
