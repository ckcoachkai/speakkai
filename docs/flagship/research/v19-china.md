## What a school coordinator needs to decide

Keep the current SpeakKai Schools offer categories, then place a short preparation brief beside them. The brief should collect decision information rather than force a package choice:

1. **Audience:** age range, year group, approximate group size, and language level.
2. **Need:** speaking, storytelling, debate, presentation, teacher modelling, or feedback practice.
3. **Setting:** timetable, one-off workshop or lesson sequence, room or online setup, and preferred dates.
4. **People:** coordinator, participating teachers, student supervision, and who will make decisions.
5. **Success definition:** the communication task or learning focus the school wants to explore; do not promise a result.
6. **Boundaries:** learning materials, safeguarding requirements, recording or public-sharing permissions, and any reporting request.

The first inquiry can omit student names and individual records. A school can provide enough context to discuss fit without treating the first contact as a booking.

## Responsibility boundaries

**School or coordinator provides:** context, age and group information, timetable constraints, room or online requirements, staff and supervision arrangements, safeguarding rules, and any recording or sharing permissions the school would require.

**Kai discusses and confirms:** whether a workshop, lesson sequence, or teacher-development conversation fits; possible aims, participants, format, duration, materials, fees, dates, availability, and any reporting or sharing terms.

**Joint confirmation is required before action:** scope, learning focus, participants, delivery format, timetable, responsibilities, fees, availability, and any handling of student work. A public page should not imply that a school partnership, standard package, booking, place, deliverable, or outcome already exists.

## Concise structure for the current Schools page

Use four short sections:

1. **Choose the conversation** — `A focused workshop`, `A sequence of lessons`, `Teacher development`. Give each one sentence describing the planning question it answers.
2. **Prepare a short brief** — `Age / year group`, `Group size`, `Communication need`, `Preferred dates`, `Delivery setting`, and `Staff or safeguarding requirements`.
3. **What we confirm together** — scope, participants, format, timetable, responsibilities, materials, fees, availability, and permissions.
4. **Start a school conversation** — `Share your age range, approximate group size, learning goal, preferred dates and delivery format. A first inquiry does not need student names or individual records.`

Use one neutral CTA such as `Discuss a school program`. Keep `Workshop`, `Lesson sequence`, and `Teacher development` as discussion routes, not purchasable packages. Do not add partner logos, school names, case studies, booking language, fixed deliverables, prices, or outcome statistics without separate verification.
