## Translation choices and scope checks

- “Public speaking” is translated as `公众表达` in the location line and `演讲` where the surrounding offer is specifically speech or debate. This keeps the Chinese natural without narrowing every service to competition speaking.
- “Communication coach” is `沟通教练`; “speaker” is `演讲者`; “executive coaching” is `高管辅导`. These are direct role descriptions, not added credentials.
- “Growing independence” is `逐渐增强的独立性`, retaining the gradual meaning and avoiding a guarantee of confidence or competition results.
- The Chinese draft keeps `Fall 2026 Young Competition Speakers` and its confirmed `Grades 1–2 / 18 classes` wording in the existing offer data; those fields were not silently expanded or reinterpreted here.
- `学校项目` is used for the homepage card’s general “programs”; on the offer page, `项目范围` remains the scope/engagement meaning. If the UI needs mainland-China consistency, `课程项目` can replace the card label without changing the promise.
