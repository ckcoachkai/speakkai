## SpeakKai application

Parents, schools, and companies arrive with different decisions. The Chinese page should make the route explicit before asking for details:

- **Parent / student:** what the learner is preparing for, such as everyday speaking, a competition, or a specific event; age or grade, goal, timing, and preferred format.
- **School / educator:** learner age or grade, language level, group size, timetable, format, and staff or safeguarding requirements. No student names are needed for an initial fit discussion.
- **Company / event team:** organization type, audience, communication challenge, participant count, date range, format, and relevant constraints.

Recommended non-copying Chinese headings:

- `适合谁`
- `你可能正在准备什么`
- `可以先准备这些信息`
- `讨论下一步`

Intent-specific contact labels can be:

- `讨论学生辅导`
- `讨论学校项目`
- `讨论演讲或培训`

These labels describe a conversation. They do not imply a free trial, fixed package, booking, confirmed place, or guaranteed outcome. Do not copy `免费预约试听` or `马上领取试听课`: SpeakKai has no verified trial or booking funnel in this benchmark.

## Responsibility and offer boundaries

The visitor can provide an audience, goal or need, approximate group size, timing, format, and relevant constraints. Kai can then discuss fit, scope, format, fees, dates, and availability. A partnership, booking, fixed package, deliverable, venue, or outcome is only established after confirmation.

Keep the confirmed program facts separate from the audience-entry pattern: **Young Competition Speakers; Fall 2026; Grades 1–2; 18 classes; six three-class units; 60–90-second practice speeches; and a supported three-minute final showcase.** Do not attach a fee, capacity, school partnership, certificate, competition result, or guarantee to those facts without fresh evidence.
