## SpeakKai hypothesis

Create a clearly fictional illustration: **yellow umbrella, blue notebook, park bench**. Keep the text-first alternative beside it. Use three prompts:

1. `你看见了什么？只说画面中能确认的细节。`
2. `你觉得可能发生了什么？把猜想说成“可能……”。`
3. `如果把它讲成一个故事，下一句会是什么？`

Label the image `虚构练习示例 · 中文文字版` and avoid a real place, identifiable person, testimonial, or claimed learner result. A static card or printable sheet should show the same image description and prompts for visitors who do not use the illustration. Do not present an inference as an observed fact. The source is a resource directory; linked lesson pages, image rendering, and classroom outcomes were not tested.
