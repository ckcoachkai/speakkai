## Strengths and gaps for SpeakKai

**Strengths to learn from:** the visitor can choose a role/use case before submitting a brief; each CTA promises a recognizable next artifact (a programme, plan, or quote); the content explains why a generic course library is insufficient; and the enterprise form captures enough organization context for a first conversation.

**Gaps:** the reference is a broad corporate learning catalogue. It does not show a speaking-specific practice cycle, speaker/listener roles, a rehearsal artifact, confidentiality handling for internal presentations, or a clear distinction between a keynote, group workshop, and individual coaching. The general claims, course counts, and response promise are publisher claims; the forms were not submitted, so follow-up behavior is unverified.

## Concrete V08 recommendation: three explicit resource doors

Keep the flagship home and contact surface organized around three visible doors, each with a useful resource before or beside the inquiry:

1. **学生与家长｜先试一个表达练习**
   - Resource: a clearly labelled illustrative speaking starter with prompt, short attempt, one observable adjustment, retry, and parent-readable recap.
   - Entry fields: language, age/grade, speaking goal, event deadline, preferred format.
   - Next step: discuss coaching fit. Do not imply a competition result or collect school records.

2. **学校与教育工作者｜索取学校项目简介**
   - Resource: one-page programme brief with age range, language level, group size, lesson length, learning focus, sample task/rubric, materials, safeguarding/supervision questions, and reporting options.
   - Entry fields: age range, approximate group size, learning goal, preferred dates, delivery format, staff responsibilities.
   - Next step: discuss a workshop, lesson sequence, or teacher-development scope. Do not imply a fixed package until agreed.

3. **企业与活动团队｜提交一个沟通场景**
   - Resource: a short decision-ready-update or presentation-rehearsal template showing message, audience, evidence, delivery choice, and next rehearsal.
   - Entry fields: organization type, audience, communication challenge, approximate participants, date range, format, and confidentiality constraints.
   - Next step: discuss keynote, practical team workshop, or presentation/executive coaching. An inquiry or schedule view does not confirm a booking.

This is a content architecture recommendation, not a claim that 时代光华’s labels or calls to action will perform for SpeakKai. Use direct descriptive links so the visitor understands the resource and audience before opening a form.

## Adult group-speaking practice pattern

For the company door, adapt the source’s role/use-case routing into a small practice sequence:

`name the business situation → state the audience decision → make a short update → listener records one understood point and one question → speaker retries with one adjustment`.

The teacher or facilitator frames the task and keeps feedback observable. Listener notes should describe comprehension, not rank participants. A private recap can record the next rehearsal priority without a public score, leaderboard, “best speaker” label, or comparison table. This provides a concrete adult practice experience while keeping corporate material confidential.

## Content fields that keep the doors editable

Use the established lesson contract for `language + audience`, `timing`, `ordered steps`, `feedback/next action`, and `source/review status`. Add:

- `resourceType`: practice starter, school programme brief, or company scenario template;
- `entryQuestion`: the one question that identifies the visitor’s situation;
- `deliveryChoice`: online, in-person, hybrid, or to be discussed;
- `privacyNote`: what not to send in the first inquiry and whether recordings are off by default;
- `nextStepLabel`: try the resource, request the brief, or discuss fit and scope.
