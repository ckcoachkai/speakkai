## Recommended fictional paired speaking example

Recommended location: **Resources**, under a label such as `教学示例` or `配对练习示例`. If placed in **Companies**, label it `虚构教学示例`, never a client case or customer result.

Use this heading and boundary copy:

> **虚构教学示例｜先说一次，再改一处**
>
> 以下内容是为说明练习流程而写的虚构示例，不是真实学生案例，也不代表学习结果。

```text
初始简述
“我想介绍学校里的图书角。那里有很多书，我喜欢在那里看书。”

一次针对性修改
把“很多书”换成一个可看见的细节，并补充一个动作：
“图书角靠窗，第二层有一本蓝色恐龙书。我每周五先读那一本，再去找新的书。”

倾听者问题
“你为什么总是先读那本蓝色恐龙书？”
```

The sequence is intentionally narrow: initial brief → one observable detail and action → one listener question. It does not score the speaker, promise improvement, or imply that the second version was more successful. A short follow-up line may say `再讲一次时，可以保留这一个细节，也可以换成自己的细节。` Do not add a success claim.

## Editorial and placement guidance

- Keep the three stages visually adjacent so a parent or learner can understand the process without opening another page.
- Use neutral labels: `初始简述`, `一次针对性修改`, and `倾听者问题`. Avoid `前后对比成果`, `提升证明`, or `学生进步案例`.
- Keep the example original and fictional. Do not attach a student name, image, school, date, score, parent quote, or testimonial.
- Pair the demonstration with the existing confirmed course context only: **Young Competition Speakers, Fall 2026, Grades 1–2, 18 classes, six three-class units, 60–90-second practice speeches, and a supported three-minute final showcase**.
- The example is a teaching illustration, not an additional course unit, a guarantee, or a statement that every learner follows this exact sequence.
- Keep any direct contact route separate from the example. The resource should explain the practice pattern; it should not create a booking or contact claim.
