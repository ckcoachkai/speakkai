## Recommendation for SpeakKai's homepage

The current homepage already separates `Students & parents`, `Schools & educators`, and `Companies & event teams`, with broad links to `/coaching/`, `/schools/`, and `/companies/`. Add one compact discovery strip after those audience cards or near the existing method section:

> **See it in practice**  
> One short example for each audience. Open the detail page to see the full exercise or conversation.

Use three cards with one concrete action, one detail link, and one status boundary:

1. **Students & parents — Bilingual pause and retry**  
   `Read the same two sentences again, with one pause before the final sentence.`  
   Link: `See the feedback example` / `查看反馈示例` → the existing Young Competition Speakers course page.  
   Boundary: `Fictional practice example · not a student testimonial` / `虚构练习示例 · 并非学生评价`.

2. **Companies & event teams — Revise the ending**  
   `Finish a one-minute update with a clear next action or question.`  
   Link: `See the decision-ready update` / `查看沟通示例` → the existing Companies detail route.  
   Boundary: `Illustrative communication exercise · not a client result` / `沟通练习示例 · 并非客户成果`.

3. **Schools & educators — Explain, listen, swap, retry**  
   `Explain one idea, let a partner name one detail and ask one useful question, then try one clearer version.`  
   Link: `Open the free practice` / `打开免费练习` → the existing `Explain it, then swap` resource.  
   Boundary: `Free example activity · not a complete class or assessment` / `免费示例练习 · 不是完整课程或评估`.

These cards should summarize the action, not reproduce the full lesson. The detail link should open the existing page where timings, prompts, listener guidance, and materials can be read. Use one primary link per card and keep the audience label visible so the examples are easy to scan.

## Claim and responsibility boundaries

- Call the cards `example`, `exercise`, or `practice`; do not call them proof, results, case studies, or testimonials.
- Keep the current experience statements and any future examples separate. A concrete exercise shows what a learner may try; it does not validate a teaching outcome.
- Do not imply that every student, school, or company follows the example exactly. The full course or program scope remains a separate discussion.
- Preserve the existing direct contact routes. A detail link is a read-only discovery step and does not book a lesson, reserve a place, create a partnership, or confirm availability.
- Keep the confirmed Young Competition Speakers facts separate: **Fall 2026, Grades 1–2, 18 classes, six three-class units, 60–90-second practice speeches, and a supported three-minute final showcase**.
