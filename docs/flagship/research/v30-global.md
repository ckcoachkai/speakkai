## Original SpeakKai printable visual practice

Create one HTML-first sheet, with an optional browser print action or PDF export. Include:

- title, audience, estimated time and simple materials;
- a visible **AI-generated fictional scene** label and attribution;
- a full text description: *A small stage has one speaker mark, a simple lectern and three empty audience positions labelled left, centre and right. One spotlight falls on the speaker area. No people, logos or identifiable venue appear.*;
- three numbered prompts: **Observe** (name visible details only), **Imagine** (offer one possibility using “might” or “could”), **Speak** (state the possibility without presenting it as fact);
- a listener check asking for one visible detail and one phrase that clearly marks uncertainty;
- writing space for the learner’s sentence and one next adjustment.

The visual should support the sheet rather than contain essential instructions. Keep the fictional boundary beside the image and in the printed text. A static sheet must remain usable if the image fails, JavaScript is disabled or the visitor prints in black and white.
