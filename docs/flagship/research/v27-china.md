## SpeakKai hypothesis

Label an original Blender stage diorama clearly:

```text
虚构练习示例 · 中文文字版 · 无旁白 · 约 18 秒
```

Use three simple stage beats and these prompts:

1. `选择站位：你站在哪里，听众最容易看见并听清你？`
2. `面对听众：身体和视线朝向谁？`
3. `先停一下：开口前，在哪个位置停一拍？`

Keep the diorama generic: floor marks, a listener area, and simple objects can illustrate choice and attention without representing a real school or venue. Pair it with `阅读文字提示` or `打开练习单`, preserving the same three prompts for visitors who do not play motion. Do not claim that a position guarantees audibility, confidence, or audience response. The source is a resource directory; linked lesson pages, PDF rendering, playback, and real-room acoustics were not tested.
