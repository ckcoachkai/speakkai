# Version 19 — A clearer school program conversation

Status: release candidate. Entering champion V18: 44.25/55.

## Research before edits

Fresh official Cambridge English China teaching support and Toastmasters Youth Leadership Program research showed how institutional visitors benefit from distinct program directions and concrete scoping information. Sources and limits are recorded in research/v19-china.md and v19-global.md. Their qualifications, packages, ages, capacities and coordinator models are not SpeakKai facts. No new social-platform evidence was available; prior access limitations remain explicit.

Hypothesis: the existing school directions become easier to act on when each offers a specific preparation prompt and an editable inquiry handoff that preserves the selected direction.

## Implementation

The three existing format cards retain their scope and add distinct preparation prompts plus links to the contact composer. A shared panel covers timetable, setup, staff and safeguarding requirements, materials, responsibilities, fees and availability. No fixed package, result or booking is introduced.

Program direction is a school-only allowlisted enum, separate from delivery preference. It is optional and editable. Unknown query values and directions attached to non-school initial audiences are ignored. Drafts remain local and manually copied. Changing detail fields announces that the draft is unchanged; rebuilding explicitly replaces message edits. A reviewer identified and root fixed initial hidden state from a non-school URL.

Astro remains appropriate: static cards and a small existing contact script satisfy the requirement without a new framework, server or persistence layer.

## Validation

Production build: 49 pages, zero errors/warnings, seven existing hints. Twelve-route links/metadata, SEO, paired course content, published practice, schedule privacy and byte checks passed. Twenty-one inquiry and speech tests passed. Contact referenced JavaScript remains below the 8 KB static-route budget.

Browser: all three real school links selected the correct direction and generated it exactly once. Online delivery remained separate. An edited draft was copied exactly; changing audience preserved edits with a stale-draft notice, while explicit rebuild excluded the school direction. Invalid prototype query values produced a blank direction. All twelve routes fit at actual 320px and 1440px viewport widths. Schools and contact also fit doubled precomputed font sizes at 305px client width. Desktop/mobile school cards and mobile contact selector were visually reviewed. Enter activated generation. Without JavaScript, the composer remained hidden and the full direct WeChat path stayed available. Temporary emulations were restored.

Independent source review found no P0–P2 issues and supported promotion after live checks. Native zoom, screen readers, other engines, field performance and actual coordinator comprehension/conversion remain NOT_CHECKED or NOT_MEASURED. No school inquiry was sent.

## Critique and champion comparison

Improved: school visitors can compare practical starting directions and carry one into a draft without retyping it. Average: the cards remain text-heavy, and school proof is limited to first-party descriptions and a sample exercise. Ahead of a generic contact funnel: preparation and selection stay connected. Behind established institutions: there are no verified school case studies or independently observed program outcomes.
