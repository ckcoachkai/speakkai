# Version 23 — Chinese audience offers

Status: release candidate. Entering champion V22: 45.25/55.

## Research before edits

Fresh Acadsoc audience lanes demonstrate concise audience/need/action grouping; Toastmasters Membership shows concrete experience previews and distinct participation choices. Their packages and outcome claims do not transfer to SpeakKai. See research/v23-china.md and v23-global.md.

Hypothesis: translating the complete offer decision path lets Chinese visitors inspect formats, examples, scope and questions without switching to English midway through the offer.

## Implementation

Three new Chinese offers use the shared English template and a typed parallel data set. All nine formats, audience fit, practice directions, examples, twelve FAQs and next-step briefs are translated. The company example preserves the same fictional opening across both attempts and changes only the ending. School direction links keep their allowlisted query context. Remaining English contact, schedule and practice destinations are explicitly labeled. Language pairs, homepage links, breadcrumbs, metadata and sitemap include the new pages.

Astro remains suitable: this extension needs static localized pages, not an application server. No Next.js migration trigger is met. CMS publication gating remains in the shared renderer; translated page copy is source-managed, while practice content and current-course editorial content retain their existing editor workflow.

## Validation and review

Build: 53 pages, zero errors/warnings, seven existing hints. All ten existing check commands pass, including sixteen routes, six language pairs, SEO, course/editorial/practice checks, 21 inquiry/Speak tests, privacy and unchanged budgets. New language checks verify three formats per offer, disclosure count parity, school query values and confirmation/confidentiality boundaries. Source review found no P0–P2 structural issue. Language review found an incorrectly defaulted breadcrumb language tag and several phrasing issues; all recommended P2 fixes were applied and rebuilt.

All sixteen routes fit 320px and 1440px. All three Chinese offers fit doubled precomputed fonts at 305px client width. Desktop company hero and expanded example, mobile school formats, coaching FAQ and contact context were visually inspected. All three language pairs navigate in both directions, including keyboard Enter. The Chinese homepage company example reaches the translated anchor. School sequence inquiry reaches the English form with schools/sequence selected and delivery blank. The company revision opens with JavaScript disabled. Temporary emulations were restored.

## Critique and champion comparison

Improved: Chinese offer pages carry the actual decision details instead of ending at homepage summaries. Average: general inquiry and two guided practices still interrupt the language journey. Ahead of thin translated landing pages: full examples, FAQs and scope distinctions remain inspectable. Behind a fully bilingual service: human comprehension testing and independently attributable client proof remain unavailable. Proper-name language tagging and duplicated course-summary facts remain minor maintenance follow-ups.
