# Version 03 — A useful first conversation

Status: RESEARCHED, implementation in progress. Entering champion: V02, 36/55, live verified.

## Research before implementation — 2026-09-07

Duarte's https://www.duarte.com/help-contact/contact-us/ returned 200; its goal-led training/contact structure helps visitors frame a communication need. Additional global audit covers Simon Sinek and Vinh Giang contact forms. Strength: clear inquiry purpose and role routing. Gap for a small personal coaching relationship: substantial forms can ask for details before the visitor understands the next step.

https://www.21tb.com/ returned 200 and exposes trial and information-request actions, a phone field and WeChat customer-service configuration. Strength: clear next actions for enterprise buyers. SpeakKai can offer a smaller, audience-aware brief and visible WeChat handoff. Do not infer real conversion from these design patterns. Previous WeChat/Bilibili evidence and inaccessible Xiaohongshu/Douyin/Zhihu limits remain in the research ledger; no channel performance is claimed.

Hypothesis: a parent, school or company can carry their context from an offer page into a short editable inquiry and successfully copy it for WeChat, with an honest failure path and direct contact available without JavaScript. This replaces the generic contact-only destination. No backend, stored inquiries or confirmed bookings.

## Implementation and review

Replaced the legacy contact page with the flagship layout, complete QR, direct-contact shortcut and optional inquiry composer. Validated audience context is carried from offer/course pages. Optional goal, learner/group, format and timing fields generate an editable message. Clipboard success follows the actual write; denial selects the text and explains manual copying. No form submission, backend or storage is added. Astro plus a small client script; no React in V1–5.

What improved: the first conversation now has useful context and a tested action; direct WeChat remains available without JavaScript. What feels average: long mobile form, no bilingual contact, no independent proof. Ahead: a parent or buyer can prepare a relevant brief without supplying identity or a phone number. Behind the benchmark brands: limited social proof, no speaking reel and no measured handoff conversion. Those gaps are not cured by interface copy.

Validation: build passed, 42 routes, zero errors and six pre-existing hints. Static integrity passed on /, /coaching/, /schools/, /companies/, /contact/ (82 local targets). Eighteen tests passed, including four inquiry tests and fourteen existing Speak engine tests; schedule privacy passed. CUA Chromium: parent preselection and conditional label, keyboard Enter generation, exact edited-message clipboard result, denied clipboard selection/fallback, school/company/invalid query defaults, no console errors. Geometry passed at actual widths 320/768/1440. Desktop and 390px screenshots reviewed. Mobile direct-contact shortcut shows the full QR. With scripting disabled the composer is absent and QR, handle, instructions and schedule remain available. Temporary failure injection and scripting emulation were restored. Other engines, actual screen readers, 200% text enlargement and field performance: NOT_CHECKED; conversion: NOT_MEASURED.

Proposed scores in category order: 3.5,2,3.5,4,4,4,3.5,3.5,3,3,4 = 38/55, versus 36/55. Credibility and performance remain unchanged. Observable target met: audience context and an editable copyable brief are available, including failure/no-JS paths. Promotion requires final public verification.
