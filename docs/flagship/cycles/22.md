# Version 22 — A visible language choice and Chinese homepage

Status: release candidate. Entering champion V21: 45/55.

## Research before edits

Fresh British Council China bilingual routes, WHO language controls and W3C page-language guidance support direct, named language links and truthful equivalent-page metadata. See research/v22-china.md and v22-global.md. A splash adds a step without solving missing translations; the chosen top links expose the visitor's choice immediately.

Hypothesis: a real Chinese homepage and predictable top English/中文 links improve access for Chinese readers without misleading them about untranslated routes.

## Implementation

One shared homepage template has English and Chinese copy. A native top switch links three genuine pairs: home, current course and one-object-story. Other flagship routes visibly offer 中文首页; those fallbacks are excluded from hreflang. Chinese navigation, brand, breadcrumbs and footer point to Chinese equivalents where available, and explicitly label English destinations. No language preference storage or automatic redirect is introduced. Course numerical scope remains shared; the portrait variants remain intact.

## Validation and review

Build: 50 pages, zero errors/warnings, seven existing hints. Thirteen-route link/anchor and SEO audits, three reciprocal language pairs, course/editorial/practice checks, 21 inquiry/Speak tests, schedule privacy and unchanged byte budgets pass. Read-only architecture review found no P0–P2 defects. Chinese copy review prompted clearer audience and QR labels, natural student wording and corrected unit phrasing; no new claims were found.

All thirteen routes fit 320px and 1440px viewports. Both homepages fit doubled precomputed font sizes at 305px client width. Desktop and mobile layout, Chinese full-page sections, exact course/practice switches in both directions, English school fallback, keyboard language activation, Chinese course entry and contact paths were inspected. With JavaScript disabled, the native Chinese menu opens, its work anchor navigates correctly, and English switching succeeds. Temporary font and script emulations were restored.

Human translation/comprehension testing, screen-reader pronunciation, native zoom, other browser engines, field performance and conversion remain unverified. Script-disabled menu links work but do not auto-collapse after anchor navigation.
