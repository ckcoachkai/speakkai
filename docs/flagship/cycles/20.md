# Version 20 — Discover the method through examples

Status: release candidate. Entering champion V19: 44.5/55.

## Research before edits

Fresh official Hujiang/Hitalk research showed a concise learning-process summary with routes to more detail. CCL's program index exposed short fit/method previews and explicit exploration routes. Both are publisher-presented marketing sources, not proof of SpeakKai outcomes. See research/v20-china.md and v20-global.md for exact observations and unavailable evidence.

Hypothesis: homepage visitors can inspect the method more easily with direct links to an existing example for their audience, without duplicating full lessons or adding hydration.

## Implementation

A compact navigation block sits inside the existing method section. It links to the English and Chinese course pause/retry, the published school partner activity and the company ending revision. Adjacent descriptions distinguish fictional worked examples from an activity. The school's title and 12-minute duration come from the published CMS record; unpublishing that record removes its link.

The static audit now checks cross-page fragment targets, including the parent and company anchors. It decodes serialized attribute entities before URL parsing: an initial check exposed Astro's encoded query ampersand as a false fragment, which was corrected before release. No runtime, framework or data service was added. Brand and SEO strategy working references were prepared for the final handover.
