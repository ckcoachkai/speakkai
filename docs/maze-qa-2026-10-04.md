# Dynamite Mice — 50-angle QA and five-lens review

Date: 2026-10-04. Target: https://speakkai.com/maze/. Baseline e37d0e24; repaired standalone v5.1.

**44 checks passed, including repaired defects; 4 have verification limits and 2 retain performance/delivery concerns. This is not a claim of 50 unconditional passes.**

The work included 100 generated-maze graph audits, 60 completed seeded races, 10,000 explosion draws, 700 cumulative lice attachments, 40 browser resets, six viewport widths, Chromium/Chrome/Edge, failure injection and renderer A/B measurements. The 48 legacy races deliberately accelerate actors and disable lice; the 12 added races use complete production rules at 1%, 100% and 1/20/80 mice. Together they selected 1,556 names without duplicates or incomplete races.

## Confirmed defects repaired

1. Long names could split emoji. Truncation now respects grapheme clusters where Intl.Segmenter is available, with a code-point fallback.
2. CSV names beginning with formula characters could execute as spreadsheet formulas. Export now prefixes those cells and preserves Unicode and CSV quoting.
3. Invalid or missing music silently failed while the UI reported success. The game now reports the error and uses its built-in score, while preserving speaker pauses and ignoring stale track errors. Switching back to the procedural score resets its scheduling clock.
4. A speaker selected while mobile settings were visible could have an offscreen Continue button. Selection now brings its card into view without changing maze zoom.
5. Dense persistent marks were redrawn unnecessarily. Cached blood composition and sized prop frames reduce this cost; offscreen and overlapping prop draws are skipped without deleting collision objects.
6. Music upload was absent from keyboard navigation. The file control is now focusable, with visible focus styling.
7. An aria-live HUD was repeatedly updated during animation. Continuous HUD announcements were removed; selection dialog/results remain available as text. The footer now accurately says names stay in the browser.

## Five review lenses

| Lens | Finding | Challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Game rules and fairness | Connected mazes, fixed deadlines, one-time selections, cheese and lice rules pass. | This is a playful picker, not a certified lottery. Exit/cat outcomes depend on speed and routes; same-tick outcomes can depend on actor update order. The explosion distribution test alone does not prove all selection modes equally likely. |
| Reliability | Complete 80-name runs, state transitions, resets and missing-media recovery pass. | Refresh does not restore an unfinished race or result ledger. Roster and cheese preference persist; the run itself does not. |
| Performance | Identical 80-mouse/8,012-prop/2,600-splat scene improved from 56.7 to 21.8 ms mean render time. Forty resets showed no listener growth. | 4x CPU emulation was choppy, around 9–10 FPS, even at 20 names. These Windows headless measurements are not physical phone benchmarks; GPU memory was not profiled. |
| Presenter usability and accessibility | Mobile selection recovery, keyboard upload, zoom, settings and visible selected names pass. | In overview the characters are necessarily small. Keyboard/text results work, but the canvas is primarily visual. Manual screen-reader use, full WCAG contrast and physical device ergonomics need human/device testing. |
| Privacy and security | Names render as text, exported formulas are neutralized, and no roster/music upload requests were observed. | Names are stored in unencrypted localStorage on a shared browser. Site hosting still receives ordinary page/asset requests. Local storage is convenience, not an access-control system. |

## Design challenges

- At +100% cheese, many sampled mice escaped before 30 seconds, so the cat at 60 seconds often never matters. Keep +1% as the suspense default; the upper slider is an intentional chaos setting.
- One +1% cheese offsets roughly 100 attachments at −0.01% each. Individual lice are intentionally subtle under the requested mathematics; the visual crowd is more dramatic than the speed penalty.
- The media payload is 12.93 MB. The measured fresh live load was 4.5 seconds on this connection, with 600-second cache headers. A slow connection can take longer.
- A loaded game survives connection loss, but a fresh offline navigation is not supported. Use the self-contained v5.1 HTML for a reliable offline event.
- Chrome and Edge both pass, but they share the Chromium engine. Firefox, Safari/WebKit, physical iOS/Android, vibration and manual screen-reader output remain unverified.
