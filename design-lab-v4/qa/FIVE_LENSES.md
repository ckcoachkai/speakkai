# Five-Lens Review

## 1. Creative Director

- Does the concept have a coherent visual thesis?
- Does the screenshot look meaningfully different at thumbnail size?
- Is the portrait treatment intentional?
- Is motion part of the idea rather than decoration?
- Would this survive comparison with a professional design proposal?

## 2. Stakeholder / Conversion

Evaluate the declared primary stakeholder.

### Parent
- trust
- clarity
- realistic outcome
- professionalism
- next step

### Student
- energy
- belonging
- aspiration
- modernity
- willingness to explore

### Customer / School
- capability
- clarity of offering
- credibility
- adaptability
- partnership CTA

## 3. UX & Accessibility

- first-frame comprehension
- interaction discoverability
- keyboard/touch
- focus/contrast
- responsive reflow
- reduced motion
- no hidden critical information

## 4. Engineering & Performance

- component isolation
- route splitting
- media loading
- animation cost
- maintainability
- error handling
- QA selectors
- no accidental global CSS leakage

## 5. Skeptical Competitor

Attack the design:

- Is this just a trendy skin?
- Is it generic education marketing?
- Is the headline vague?
- Is the portrait doing all the work?
- Is the interface a gimmick?
- Is evidence weak?
- Is the CTA premature?
- Is it slower than the idea is worth?
- Does it alienate the primary stakeholder?
- Which other test already does this better?

The integrator decides which criticism matters and documents the fix.
