# Single-Screen Content Contract

“All the information is on one screen” should not mean placing every paragraph visibly at once.

For desktop Tests 21–50, the visitor must be able to understand or discover the full core proposition without normal vertical scrolling.

## Visible at first load

Every test must show:

1. **SpeakKai identity**
2. **Coach Kai identity**, usually through the approved portrait
3. **A clear value proposition**
4. **At least one audience/service cue**
5. **At least one trust/proof cue**
6. **A clear primary CTA**
7. **A route back to the Test 1–50 navigator**

## Reachable within one interaction

The page should make these reachable through tabs, cards, drawers, hotspots, audience modes, or another accessible device:

- main program/service categories
- teaching or coaching process
- credible outcomes/evidence
- contact or booking path
- stakeholder-specific explanation

## Copy limits

Use concise copy as an engineering constraint:

- headline: generally 4–12 words
- supporting statement: generally 12–30 words
- card heading: 2–7 words
- card explanation: generally 8–24 words
- visible proof items: 1–3
- primary CTAs: 1
- optional secondary CTA: at most 1
- interactive zones: generally 3–6

Longer content may appear in an in-viewport drawer or accessible dialog, but the homepage should not become a modal document reader.

## Stakeholder contract

### Parent

The page should answer:

- Why should I trust this?
- What could my child practise or improve?
- Is the approach thoughtful and professional?
- What is the next step?

### Student

The page should answer:

- Does this feel interesting and relevant?
- Can I imagine myself becoming more confident or capable?
- Does Coach Kai feel human and engaging?
- What can I try next?

### Customer / school / organization

The page should answer:

- What can SpeakKai deliver?
- Why is SpeakKai credible?
- Can the program fit our context?
- How do we start a conversation?

Each test may specialize, but it must succeed for its declared primary stakeholder.

## Accuracy

Never fabricate:

- schools
- partners
- testimonials
- student outcomes
- numbers
- awards
- affiliations
- credentials
- dates
- availability
- pricing

Create `content/verification-report.md` before using uncertain claims.
