# 30-Concept Design Map

## Tests 21–30 — Corporate / Trust / Institutional

| Test | Concept | Primary stakeholder | Core visual idea | Main interaction |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | **Boardroom Editorial** | School / organizational customer | Asymmetric broadsheet: oversized statement, cropped portrait column, compact program brief and proof rail. | Three briefing tabs switch Programs, Evidence, and Partnership without leaving the viewport. |
| 22 | **Swiss Evidence Grid** | Parent | Twelve-column modular grid with a small identity block, one large question, and evidence tiles of unequal scale. | Evidence tiles expand in place on click/focus; one tile can be open at a time. |
| 23 | **Annual Report Live** | School / organizational customer | Live report cover with a CEO-letter portrait, a program ring, a process timeline, and a compact evidence drawer. | Hover/focus on report sections previews a concise summary; click opens a side sheet. |
| 24 | **Executive Keynote** | Adult customer / event organizer | One dominant slide at a time with a visible four-chapter rail: Why, Programs, Proof, Contact. | Click, keyboard arrows, or chapter rail swaps the in-viewport slide state. |
| 25 | **Parent Decision Matrix** | Parent | Four-quadrant concern matrix with a portrait anchor and a dynamic recommendation panel. | Choose Confidence, Structure, Competition, or Presentation; content and CTA adapt instantly. |
| 26 | **Institutional Blueprint** | School / organizational customer | Architectural plan with labeled zones for audience, format, curriculum, delivery, and outcomes. | Click blueprint zones to reveal implementation details in a compact inspector. |
| 27 | **Coaching Atelier** | Premium private client / parent | Editorial salon: framed portrait, one elegant thesis, three service swatches, discreet proof drawer. | Service swatches reveal bespoke coaching pathways and examples. |
| 28 | **Case Study Wall** | Parent | Layered case cards with a coach annotation column and a fixed evidence/CTA spine. | Select a case card to reveal Goal → Practice → Change → Next step. |
| 29 | **Documentary Press Room** | Parent | Full-viewport photo contact sheet with a central story caption and a vertical evidence ticker. | Focus/click a frame to enlarge it and reveal one factual story sentence. |
| 30 | **Corporate Motion Identity** | Customer / event organizer | Full-screen typographic identity system with portrait slices, moving rule architecture, and a fixed action dock. | Hover/focus over Speaking, Debate, TEDx, or Business shifts the identity composition and copy. |

## Tests 31–40 — Fun / Animated / Motion-Graphic

| Test | Concept | Primary stakeholder | Core visual idea | Main interaction |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 31 | **Skill Galaxy** | Student | Coach Kai at the center with orbiting skill worlds and a lower mission dock. | Click/focus orbiting worlds for Confidence, Structure, Debate, Performance, and Leadership. |
| 32 | **Stage Quest** | Student | Isometric or layered stage with four visible levels and a coach guide panel. | Select Level 1–4 to reveal goals, practice, challenge, and showcase. |
| 33 | **Comic Speech Lab** | Student | Six unequal comic panels that together form one complete 16:9 story. | Panels expand slightly and reveal mini lessons; keyboard order follows story order. |
| 34 | **Kinetic Type Jam** | Student | Giant live typography field with four control words: Power, Pitch, Pace, Pause. | Selecting each P changes type scale, rhythm, spacing, and supporting message. |
| 35 | **Speaking Game HUD** | Student | Futuristic HUD around a portrait with four non-numeric skill modules and a mission card. | Choose a skill module to see one practice mission and one outcome. |
| 36 | **Sticker Cutout Studio** | Student | Large portrait cutout surrounded by draggable but bounded stickers, labels, and mini cards. | Drag decorative stickers; click functional stickers to reveal programs and proof. |
| 37 | **Classroom Desk World** | Parent | Top-down desk scene: notebook, microphone, cue cards, trophy, calendar, and portrait polaroid. | Each object maps to a content category; selecting it opens a note card within the desk. |
| 38 | **Rhythm & Voice Visualizer** | Student | Portrait on one edge, a large waveform/rhythm field, and a compact control dock. | Select Pace, Pause, Emphasis, or Emotion to change the visualization and coaching prompt. |
| 39 | **Transformation Flipbook** | Parent | Central flipbook/cards with four stages: Unsure, Structured, Practiced, Ready; portrait and CTA frame the sequence. | Drag a timeline or use arrows to move through the four stages. |
| 40 | **Motion Poster Festival** | Student | A full-bleed poster with modular type blocks, portrait crop, event-like program strips, and one action stamp. | Hover/focus/click rearranges poster modules into Student, Parent, or School information states. |

## Tests 41–50 — Modern / Spatial / Experimental

| Test | Concept | Primary stakeholder | Core visual idea | Main interaction |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 41 | **Spatial Glass Canvas** | Parent | Three floating translucent planes: Identity, Programs, Evidence, with a grounded CTA dock. | Select a plane to bring it forward; all content remains keyboard reachable. |
| 42 | **Neo-Brutalist Studio** | Student | Hard-edged blocks, oversized headline, portrait stamp, visible utility controls, asymmetrical grid. | Big labeled blocks open as in-place panels; visible interaction affordances. |
| 43 | **3D Card Architecture** | Customer / partner | Perspective deck of four program cards, portrait as the stabilizing front plane, CTA fixed to horizon. | Keyboard/click rotates the card deck; each card exposes a concise program proposition. |
| 44 | **Lens Portal** | Mixed | Large circular/elliptical lens with portrait inside; contextual content wraps the perimeter. | Move or select the lens to focus on Coach, Programs, Students, or Partnerships. |
| 45 | **Quiet Monochrome** | Parent | One portrait, one statement, three tiny proof anchors, one CTA; disclosure appears in a precise side rail. | Three discreet anchors reveal Method, Experience, and Programs in the side rail. |
| 46 | **SpeakKai OS** | Customer / partner | Desktop canvas with four purposeful windows: Coach, Programs, Proof, Contact. | Windows can focus, minimize, and snap; keyboard order remains logical. |
| 47 | **Cinematic Hotspot Portrait** | Parent | Full-bleed portrait or speaking image with five anchored hotspots and a fixed brand/CTA strip. | Hotspots reveal concise cards for Coach, Method, Programs, Results, and Contact. |
| 48 | **Fluid Typography Field** | Student | A fluid headline occupies most of the canvas; portrait and information modules move around its changing negative space. | Audience controls morph the headline and rearrange the information field. |
| 49 | **Living Network Map** | School / organizational customer | Central SpeakKai node with four clusters: Audiences, Skills, Programs, Outcomes; portrait integrated as the human core. | Select a node to trace a real pathway, such as Student → Debate → Small Group → Confidence. |
| 50 | **Adaptive Audience Prism** | Mixed | A central portrait/prism with three audience faces; selecting one transforms the same single-screen composition. | Parent, Student, and School/Customer mode controls; state is shareable via URL parameter. |
