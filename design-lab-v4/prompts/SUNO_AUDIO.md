# Optional Suno Instrumental Briefs

Audio is optional and should be used for a design-lab showreel or explicit user-controlled sound mode only.

## Corporate family

Instrumental only. Modern keynote energy, restrained electronic pulse, warm piano or muted plucks, confident but not aggressive, around 96–104 BPM, clear rise-and-resolve structure, no vocals, no recognizable melody, suitable for an education and communication brand.

## Fun/motion family

Instrumental only. Playful kinetic electro-pop with crisp percussion, bright synth stabs, light funk rhythm, energetic but not childish, around 112–122 BPM, strong edit points for motion graphics, no vocals, no recognizable melody.

## Modern family

Instrumental only. Minimal future-facing electronic soundscape, spacious bass, clean glassy textures, subtle rhythmic pulse, around 90–110 BPM, premium and optimistic, no vocals, no recognizable melody.

## Website rules

- default muted
- explicit Sound On control
- no audio required to understand content
- store license/source information
