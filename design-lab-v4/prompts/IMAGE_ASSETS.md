# Image Asset Prompt Templates

Use the approved original Coach Kai photo as the reference image when creating portrait-derived assets.

## Universal portrait guardrails

- preserve facial identity and natural skin tone
- preserve realistic head/body proportions
- no invented awards, logos, microphones, stages, schools, or uniforms
- no text embedded into the image
- no watermark
- do not change body size unless explicitly requested
- retain generous transparent or text-safe space as specified
- export high resolution
- make the result compatible with web cropping
- keep an authentic, minimally edited version available

## Corporate derivative

Create a refined editorial portrait derivative from the supplied photo. Preserve the person accurately. Use controlled studio-style lighting, clean edge separation, and a neutral-to-navy background with optional subtle yellow accent geometry. Leave text-safe space according to the asset job. Professional, human, credible, not a fake corporate headshot.

## Fun/motion derivative

Create an accurate high-energy cutout derivative from the supplied photo. Preserve facial identity and natural proportions. Use clean transparent edges, optional graphic outline, and layered shadow shapes suitable for motion graphics. Do not make it childish or meme-like unless the specific test brief requests that style.

## Modern derivative

Create an accurate future-facing portrait derivative from the supplied photo. Preserve the face and natural skin. Use subtle optical, duotone, monochrome, halftone, or glass/lens treatment as specified, while avoiding face distortion. Keep text-safe and interaction-safe zones.

## Decorative asset rule

Generated decorative elements may be imaginative. Generated evidence may not be.
