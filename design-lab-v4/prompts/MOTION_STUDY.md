# Motion Study Prompt

Create a 1920×1080 motion study for SpeakKai Test {TEST_NUMBER}: {TITLE}.

Read the experiment specification and project visual identity first.

The study should demonstrate:

- the final hero frame
- primary entrance
- one user-triggered state transition
- reduced-motion fallback concept
- CTA emphasis
- portrait treatment
- how information remains readable

Duration: 6–10 seconds.

No autoplay audio requirement.
No infinite loops.
No random timing.
No inaccessible rasterized text.
Do not treat the rendered video as the final website; it is a timing/art-direction reference or supporting asset.

Export:

- MP4 study
- poster frame
- timing notes
- motion token notes
