# Rehearsal stage illustration

Purpose: an original fictional stage diorama supporting three optional rehearsal prompts. It is not a real SpeakKai venue, accessibility plan, client event or testimonial. The web uses a lightweight static render with descriptive alternative text; the editable scene and GLB are source deliverables.

Runtime: C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe; Blender5.2.1 LTS, build9e2066aef7ef. CPU Cycles,32 samples with denoise; AgX medium-high contrast, exposure0; PNG1200×900 final, transparent background disabled. Fixed perspective plus front/back/left/right/top evidence. No animation or external textures. No approval gates requested; self-review each stage.

Parts: one rounded dark-blue presentation plinth, raised warm wood stage with two broad steps, a slatted acoustic backdrop, lectern with supported microphone, and six chairs facing the speaker area. Gold circular standing mark is a visual cue. Chairs have distinct upholstered seats, backs and four attached legs. Camera favors the open listener side. Model footprint approximately9×9m; intended as a smooth stylized miniature rather than photoreal event evidence. Triangle ceiling150,000; use sufficient bevels and radial resolution for smooth edges, not a minimum polygon target.

Materials: dark matte plinth, lightly varied timber, brushed metal supports, blue upholstery, small gold accent. The render must show readable contacts and separation, not hide intersections in darkness. Procedural coordinates for timber; no missing textures. Export solid material approximations where procedural shading is not portable.

Stages: contract → neutral graybox/proportions with perspective/front/side/top views → primary/secondary construction → smooth bevels and contact refinement → materials → surface/presentation polish → export and fresh-import validation. Final gates: deterministic source, nonempty meshes, finite coordinates, all required parts, no unexplained floating components, matching authored/imported bounds, all artifacts open, fixed multiview evidence visually reviewed.

Review questions: Does the empty scene suggest a place to rehearse without implying a booked venue? Do chairs clearly face the stage? Is the standing mark visible? Do stage, podium and chairs feel grounded? Does the asset remain legible at mobile width without embedding instructional text in the image?
